ABOUT SAAF

Leather craftsmanship with global standards.

SAAF Leather Products is an export-focused leather manufacturing company based in Ambur, Tamil Nadu, India.

Founded in 2015, we specialize in ladies' footwear, leather bags, belts, gloves and leather apparel. Our products are mainly manufactured for customers across Australia, Europe and the United States.
