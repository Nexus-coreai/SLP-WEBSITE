ABOUT US

A leather manufacturer built for global markets.

SAAF Leather Products is an export-focused leather manufacturing company based in Ambur, Tamil Nadu, India.

Established in 2015 by Aafaque Mohammed K and Sadeed Ahmed M, the company began with a vision to build a trusted leather manufacturing business based on quality, craftsmanship and long-term customer relationships.

In 2018, Mohammed Taukhir M joined the company, strengthening our journey of growth and development.

Today, we manufacture ladies' footwear, leather bags, belts, gloves and leather apparel, mainly for customers across Australia, Europe and the United States.
